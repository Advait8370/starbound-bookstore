Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Starbound Stories Bookstore does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

1. Data Collection

The application does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal identity details. No personal data is gathered, tracked, or sent to any external server.

2. Local Storage

Any app preferences (such as light/dark mode settings, zoom level, favorites, and downloaded offline books) are stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage and are never uploaded.

3. Third-Party Services

The application fetches public book catalogs and PDF files hosted on cloud storage. No identifiers or personal browsing patterns are shared with these endpoints.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository.
